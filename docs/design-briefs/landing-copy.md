## 15 · FAQ — flip signs (owner-voiced)
1. **Will customers know it's AI?** → If they ask, it never pretends to be human. Most just notice they finally got an answer in 30 seconds instead of tomorrow.
2. **What if it doesn't know the answer?** → It says so, tells the customer you'll follow up, and flags the thread for you. It never invents prices or promises.
3. **Do I keep my phone number?** → Yes. Lumina catches what you miss — it doesn't replace your line.
4. **How long does setup take?** → About 10 minutes: your hours, services, prices, and how you like to sound. That becomes your shop's brain.
5. **What does it cost?** → Free during the pilot. When plans launch, pilot shops get first — and best — pricing.
